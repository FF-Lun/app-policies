# MD Studio — 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated：2026-11-12

## 繁體中文

**MD Studio**（以下稱「本 App」）是一個在你的裝置上離線運作的 Markdown 編輯與簡報工具。

**我們不收集任何資料。** 本 App 沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，開發者也沒有伺服器可以接收你的資料。

- **你的筆記**只儲存在你自己的裝置（本 App 的資料夾），不會上傳。
- **Python 程式碼**在你的裝置上、本 App 內建的環境執行，不會傳送到任何地方。
- **剪貼簿**只有在你按下「從剪貼簿新增」時才會讀取。
- **「問 AI」與「下載到本機」**透過 iOS 的分享選單，由你自己選擇要把內容送到哪個 App；本 App 不會自行傳送。
- **網路連線**：僅在你主動於「Python 圖表字型」下載日文／韓文／簡體中文字型時，會連線到 GitHub（raw.githubusercontent.com）下載字型檔，下載過程不含任何個人資料。其餘功能完全離線。
- 若你在筆記中加入網頁連結並點擊，會交由系統瀏覽器開啟，適用該網站自己的隱私政策。

**兒童**：本 App 不針對兒童，也不收集任何人的資料。

**變更**：若政策有更動，會更新本頁並修改上方日期。

## English

**MD Studio** ("the App") is a Markdown editor and slide tool that works offline on your device.

**We do not collect any data.** The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and the developer operates no server that could receive your data.

- **Your notes** are stored only on your device (in the App's folder) and are never uploaded.
- **Python code** runs on your device in the environment bundled with the App and is never sent anywhere.
- **The clipboard** is read only when you tap "New from clipboard".
- **"Ask AI" and "Export"** use the iOS share sheet; you choose which app receives the content. The App sends nothing by itself.
- **Network access**: only when you choose to download a Japanese / Korean / Simplified Chinese font under "Python chart fonts", the App connects to GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) to fetch the font file. No personal data is sent. All other features work fully offline.
- Links you tap in a note open in the system browser and are subject to that site's own privacy policy.

**Children**: The App is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes**: If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
